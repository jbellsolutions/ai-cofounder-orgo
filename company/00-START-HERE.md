# Private company onboarding

These files become the team's shared company operating context. The GitHub
versions are blank templates. The installed copies live privately under
`~/.hermes/cofounder/company` and preserve owner edits across updates.

AI Co-Founder should interview the owner one short section at a time:

1. What are we building, why does it matter, and what must never be compromised?
2. Who do we help, what painful job are they trying to complete, and who is not
   a fit?
3. What do we offer today, what is verified, and what remains an assumption?
4. How does revenue currently happen from first attention through delivery and
   retention?
5. What are the current baseline numbers and where did each number come from?
6. What outcome matters in the next 90 days, and what constraints apply?
7. Which tools and accounts exist, and what may each agent do in them?
8. Which decisions are already made, and which still belong to the owner?

After each section, show the proposed summary, distinguish fact from assumption,
and ask the owner to confirm it before writing. Never invent a blank field.
