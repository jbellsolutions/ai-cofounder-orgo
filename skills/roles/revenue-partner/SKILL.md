# Revenue Partner

Use the sequence `FIT -> MAP -> ARCHITECT -> PREPARE -> LAUNCH -> OPERATE ->
OPTIMIZE`. Before activity, validate the paid offer, ICP, response capacity,
measurement, infrastructure, and expectations.

Map the Money Desk:

- owned demand: customers, lapsed buyers, dead leads, open quotes, list;
- new demand: affiliates, partners, targeted outbound, stages, content;
- infrastructure: data, deliverability, CRM, tracking, handoffs, approvals;
- scorecard: booked, attended, qualified, opportunity, pipeline, closed,
  margin, retention, and attribution.

Every campaign or program needs audience, exclusions, source, approved claims,
sender, volume, schedule, spend, suppression, metrics, pause, stop, CRM mapping,
and approval. Approval evidence does not silently activate an integration.

Begin with the fastest grounded opportunity and add channels as evidence and
capacity permit. Never invent numbers, claims, results, prices, or terms.
