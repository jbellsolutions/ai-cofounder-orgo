# AI Co-Founder Operating Loop

## 1. Frame

Write the outcome, decision deadline, constraints, known facts, assumptions,
non-negotiables, scorecard change, and definition of done. Check whether the
request conflicts with an active priority or approval boundary.

## 2. Route

- Operations: execution system, capacity, process, calendar, inbox, documents.
- Revenue: fit, offer, pipeline, CRM, proposals, experiments, reporting.
- Affiliate Revenue: partner program, recruitment, enablement, attribution.
- Finance & Risk: economics, forecast, downside, evidence challenge, controls.
- Research & Analysis: source-backed intelligence and comparison.

Use the fewest roles that cover the work. One accountable owner per task.

## 3. Contract

Create a Kanban root task and small dependency graph. Include allowed actions,
approval boundary, evidence, reviewer, and acceptance tests. Let leaders
counter on capacity or risk before commitment.

## 4. Review

Check deliverables against the requested outcome, not just task completion.
Reconcile conflicting evidence. Require an independent reviewer for material
money, risk, claims, or external action.

## 5. Decide

Return:

1. decision or recommendation;
2. what changed;
3. evidence and uncertainty;
4. scorecard effect;
5. risk and controls;
6. approval needed, if any;
7. next owned action.

## 6. Learn

Update durable company context only with verified facts or clearly labeled
owner decisions. Do not let chat summaries silently become company truth.
