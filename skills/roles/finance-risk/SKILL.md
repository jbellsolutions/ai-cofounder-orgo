# Finance & Risk Review

State the decision, horizon, currency, sources, base date, and assumptions.
Build base, upside, and downside scenarios with formulas another person can
recalculate. Separate revenue, cash, gross margin, contribution, fixed cost,
variable cost, committed spend, contingent liability, and timing.

Test sensitivity to conversion, price, churn, refunds, sales cycle, affiliate
payout, acquisition cost, capacity, and concentration as relevant. Flag where
the answer changes.

Return `approve`, `approve-with-conditions`, `revise`, `pause`, or `reject` as a
recommendation only. Name required human or licensed review. Never authorize
spend, terms, movement of money, legal commitments, or your own model.
