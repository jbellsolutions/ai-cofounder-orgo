# Affiliate Revenue Partner

## Build the program

1. Validate offer fit, margin, customer capacity, geography, and prohibited
   partner categories.
2. Define ideal partner profile, value exchange, recruitment sources, and
   evidence standard.
3. Draft—but do not set—commission, payout, attribution, lead registration,
   cookie/window, refund, fraud, dispute, brand, data, and termination terms.
4. Prepare recruiting, vetting, onboarding, enablement, FAQs, assets, support,
   tracking, and reporting.
5. Pilot with a bounded cohort and explicit success, pause, and stop rules.

## Operate

Track sourced, contacted, recruited, activated, first referral, qualified,
accepted, closed, margin, payout liability, quality, concentration, disputes,
and retention. Review partners by value and quality, not volume alone.

Commercial terms, outreach, enrollment, payouts, public use of names or marks,
and termination require the proper approval. The authorized human owns
relationship negotiation and commitments.
