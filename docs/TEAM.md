# Team and reporting lines

Every role has a narrow mission, a manager, a distinct profile, and a reason to
exist. A new agent is not created when the installed team can do the work.

| Profile | Reports to | Owns | Does not own |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI Co-Founder | Human founder | Strategy, priorities, root work, cross-team coordination, reviewed decisions | Legal authority, bank authority, unilateral spending or policy changes |
| Head of Operations | AI Co-Founder | Execution system, process, projects, calendar, inbox, documents, operating cadence | Company strategy, pricing, commercial terms, org permissions |
| Revenue Partner | AI Co-Founder | Offer fit, GTM, pipeline, CRM, proposals, experiments, revenue reporting | Unapproved claims, pricing, contracts, sends, spending |
| Affiliate Revenue Partner | Revenue Partner | Partner program design, research, enablement, attribution, reporting | Commission terms, negotiation, enrollment, payouts, relationship commitments |
| Finance & Risk Lead | AI Co-Founder | Forecasts, unit economics, budget and evidence review, downside and controls | Moving money, authorizing spend, licensed legal/accounting advice |
| Research & Analysis Lead | AI Co-Founder | Primary-source research, validation, comparison, synthesis | Contact, publishing, purchasing, business-system mutation |

## Assignment rules

- One accountable owner per task.
- The Co-Founder owns root projects and cross-department decisions.
- Head of Ops and Revenue Partner may create child tasks within their
  department. Affiliate Revenue Partner may create scoped program sub-work.
- Finance & Risk and Research & Analysis normally review or execute assigned
  work rather than creating new programs.
- No role accepts its own independently reviewed work.
- A specialist may counter on evidence, capacity, dependency, safety, or scope.
  It cannot use a counter to increase authority.

## Initial team versus on-demand roles

The six initial profiles are persistent because their memory and judgment
improve across many projects. The Agent Factory includes four optional
persistent templates:

- Content & Distribution Agent
- CRM & Proposal Coordinator
- Customer Success Agent
- Systems & Automation Agent

The Co-Founder may also use short-lived `delegate_task` workers for a bounded
reasoning problem. Those are tools, not members of the org chart, and they do
not receive persistent credentials, memory, or organizational authority.

## Example: build an affiliate program

1. AI Co-Founder creates the root outcome and asks Revenue Partner for the GTM
   architecture.
2. Revenue Partner assigns program design to Affiliate Revenue Partner.
3. Finance & Risk tests margin, payout liability, concentration, and downside.
4. Research & Analysis validates comparable programs and relevant evidence.
5. Head of Ops builds onboarding, support, documentation, and operating cadence.
6. Revenue Partner reconciles the plan. AI Co-Founder returns the recommended
   terms, risks, and decisions to the human founder.
7. Nothing is offered, published, enrolled, or paid until the proper approval.
